# Reference

- **[Environment manifest](./environment):** every field of an environment's JSON, and each kind of step.
- **[URL parameters](./urls):** `env`, `db`, `storage`, `sql` and `engine`, which open the workbench on a database,
  a query or an engine.
- **[Files](./files):** what **Open…**, **Save**, the exports and the imports read and write.
- **[Keyboard shortcuts](./shortcuts):** the keys of the editors and the grids.

The languages have references of their own: SQL on [smaltql's site](https://stenhoor.github.io/smaltql/), dataframes
on [smaltdf's](https://stenhoor.github.io/smaltdf/) and Cypher on [smaltcy's](https://stenhoor.github.io/smaltcy/).
The engine, its plugins and its error codes are on [smaltdb.dev](https://smaltdb.dev).
